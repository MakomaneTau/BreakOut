# BreakOut Development Log - Executive Summary

## Quick Overview
12 videos documenting 4 weeks of development, showing the progression from initial setup to finished game.

---

## VIDEO BREAKDOWN

### WEEK 1: Foundation & Models
1. **Project Setup** - Scene, camera, renderer, skybox, basic lighting
2. **Structure Models** - Prison, stairs, platform loading and positioning  
3. **Obstacle Models** - Concrete blocks, spinning blades, lasers placement

### WEEK 2: Character & Collisions
4. **Character in Blender** - Creating Eve model with animations (idle, run, jump, roll)
5. **Character in Game** - Loading Eve, AnimationMixer, animation system
6. **Collision Start** - CollisionManager, box colliders, basic collision detection

### WEEK 3: Systems & Polish
7. **Advanced Collisions** - Dynamic obstacles, registration system, moving objects
8. **Custom Shaders** - Concrete and platform shaders with noise/patterns
9. **Lighting** - HDR environment, shadows, multiple light types

### WEEK 4: Levels & Completion
10. **Level Development** - Three levels with increasing difficulty and obstacles
11. **Performance** - Ocean removal decision (too slow, commented out not deleted)
12. **Final Polish** - Health system, timer, win condition, UI

## TECHNICAL TERMS - SIMPLE EXPLANATIONS

When you mention technical terms, always explain them:

- **Three.js** → "A library that lets us make 3D graphics in the browser"
- **GLTF** → "A file format that packages 3D models with their textures and animations"
- **Shader** → "Code that tells the computer how to color each pixel"
- **Collider** → "An invisible box we use to detect when objects touch"
- **AnimationMixer** → "A system that plays the right animation at the right time"
- **HDR** → "A high-quality image format that provides realistic lighting"

## EDITING TIPS

1. **Cut out:** Long pauses, "umms", excessive backtracking
2. **Keep in:** A few "oops" moments (makes it realistic)
3. **Add text overlays:** Key terms, code snippets, labels
4. **Show code zoomed:** When explaining specific lines
5. **Add captions:** Technical terms spelled out
6. **Transition slides:** Between major sections

---

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Too technical** - Don't use jargon without explaining  
❌ **Too fast** - Don't rush through explanations  
❌ **No mistakes** - Some mistakes make it more realistic  
❌ **Reading script** - Sound natural, not memorized  
❌ **No visuals** - Always show what you're talking about  
❌ **Skipping steps** - Show the process, not just results  

✅ **Do:** Explain simply, show everything, admit difficulties, be natural
